# Automotive Connecting Rod — Parametric CAD & FEA Validation

A mechanical engineering design project focused on the parametric CAD development and structural validation of an automotive connecting rod.

The project combines mechanical design principles, parametric 3D modeling, engineering drawing/GD&T practices, and finite element analysis.

---

## Project Overview

A connecting rod transfers reciprocating motion and combustion loads between the piston and crankshaft. It is subjected to cyclic tensile and compressive loading during engine operation.

The objective of this project is to develop a manufacturable connecting-rod geometry and evaluate its structural behavior under representative loading conditions.
